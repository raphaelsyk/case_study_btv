## execution_investment

Named products and platforms include Blackwell (ramping), Blackwell Ultra (ramping), GB300 NVL72 and GB200 NVL72, Spectrum-X Ethernet, InfiniBand, and the Vera Rubin platform (sampling, with production shipments in the second half of the year).
