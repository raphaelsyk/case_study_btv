### What internal AI use cases are named, and what is their maturity (prototype / production / scaled)?

Internal AI use cases mentioned are focused on productivity, with tools for CRM, research automation, presentation material preparation for bankers, and more efficient coding for developers. At a broader level, tools are used across operations, risk, finance, and technology teams. The company states it has 300 approved AI use cases, with 114 being live generative AI use cases, of which 34 are 'fully implemented'. This suggests a range of maturity from approved concepts to scaled production tools. Over 200,000 employees are described as 'actively using AI-enabled capabilities', generating over 400,000 prompts daily, indicating significant scaling of these tools.
